# Trading Calculators

A small collection of browser-based tools for practical trading analysis. No backend, API keys, or market-data connection required.

## Included

- **Options Payoff Calculator** — long calls/puts, vertical spreads, straddles, and strangles; estimates P&L across underlying prices and displays breakevens.
- **Position Sizing & Risk Calculator** — converts account risk and stop distance into share size, capital used, target P&L, and reward/risk.
- **Earnings Move / IV Calculator** — estimates the earnings move implied by an ATM straddle and compares it with a small sample of historical moves.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. Everything is client-side.

## Notes

These are analytical/educational tools, not investment advice. Results intentionally omit commissions, taxes, slippage, dividends, early exercise, liquidity constraints, and other market-specific effects unless explicitly modeled.
