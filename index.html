Trading Calculators

Fast, browser-only calculators for options, risk, and earnings-event analysis.

Options Payoff Calculator

Assumes standard 100-share option contracts and ignores commissions, fees, dividends, and early exercise. For educational use only.

Position Sizing & Risk

Position size is sized from the distance between entry and stop. It does not account for liquidity, slippage, gaps, or options-specific risk.

Earnings Move / IV Calculator

The straddle-implied move is approximately straddle premium ÷ stock price. A normal-model approximation for exceeding that move is shown for context; earnings returns are not normally distributed, so treat it as a rough benchmark rather than a forecast.
